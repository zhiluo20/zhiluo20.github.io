01 / How Vibeit began

Some ideas stay with you
until you make them real.

01 — The wish

A tablet full of possibilities.

My first iPad held my notes and sketches. As a student, I wanted to write Python and analyse biological data on it, too. I couldn’t find the IDE I was looking for.

02 — The decision

Build the thing I wanted.

That thought never quite left. I began turning it into Vibeit: a real workspace for code, notebooks, and scientific exploration, right on the device.

03 — The possibility

Let the hardware do more.

M-series chips bring more computing power to iPad. Vibeit gives that power a place in my everyday work, and gives an old idea a future beyond me.

02 / The other lives

For the parts of me
still becoming.

I am a molecular and computational biologist, and an Imperial College London graduate. I love science. I also wonder about the worlds I haven’t lived in: medicine, law, acting, music.

Mecury is the name I gave one of those parallel lives. In my spare time, I get to begin it — one piece of software at a time.
